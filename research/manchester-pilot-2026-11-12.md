# Sycamore UK | Manchester partnership pilot

**6 potential partners identified. 5 publish an email route. 3 to approach first.**

These are potential hosts or introducers for a financial education session. The shortlist is based on published activities and contact information. Interest, audience size and bookings are still to be confirmed.

| Start with | Why they’re worth a conversation | First ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Caribbean and African Health Network** | Co-hosted Africa Day with the University of Salford, with enterprise workshops and an external speaker. | Ask the [Africa Day team](https://www.cahn.org.uk/africa-day/) who handles partnerships and whether a session could fit an existing programme. |
| **Nigerian Community Greater Manchester** | Runs community gatherings at Nigerian House and lists a recurring general meeting. | Ask the [NCGM team](https://ncgm.org.uk/) who books guest speakers and whether there’s a slot for a practical money session. |
| **University of Salford Alumni** | The alumni team supports gatherings and has a direct contact route. | Ask the [alumni team](https://www.salford.ac.uk/alumni/reunions-and-campus-visits) whether a session on money in your first UK job would fit an alumni event. |

Three further routes are [University of Manchester Alumni Relations](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/connect/alumni/contact-us/), which runs [careers events with alumni speakers](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/connect/alumni/volunteering/speak-to-students-about-your-career/), [Igbo Community Greater Manchester](https://igbocommunitygreatermanchester.org/), which holds annual gatherings, and [Nigerian Women’s Group Manchester](https://www.nigerianwomensgroupmanchester.org/), which runs workshops and seminars and whose published phone number needs checking.

**Proposed first milestone:** approach the top three and aim to book one session. Report replies, meetings, bookings and, once a session runs, attendance and opt-ins.
